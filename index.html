THE CHALLENGE

In an emergency, finding blood
should be the easy part.

When information is spread across calls, messages and separate facilities, families can lose valuable time trying to find what they need.

01

Too many calls

Families may have to contact multiple hospitals and blood banks to ask about availability.

02

Fragmented information

Availability can be difficult to compare when information isn't gathered in one place.

03

Donors not connected

Willing donors may be nearby, but don't always know where their help is needed.

04

Trust is essential

Medical requests and transport must be handled with appropriate verification and care.

HOW IT COULD WORK

Four steps. One clearer path.

The proposed flow brings together discovery, verification and coordination — with medical teams and authorised partners at the centre.

01

Search availability

Look for a blood group and component in a selected city or area.

02

Connect nearby

Identify relevant nearby facilities and, where appropriate, eligible donors.

03

Verify the request

Support a proposed prescription upload and review process with authorised medical staff.

04

Coordinate delivery

Enable authorised partners to coordinate safe transport and share delivery status.

THE LIFELINE+ APPROACH

Technology should make care feel closer.

LifeLine+ brings the key parts of the journey into one considered experience — while leaving clinical decisions to qualified professionals.

✓

Hospital & blood-bank inventory

A shared view of availability, maintained by participating facilities.

✓

Nearby donor matching

A proposed opt-in workflow to alert eligible donors within a configurable radius.

✓

Prescription verification

Medical documentation routed securely for review by authorised personnel.

✓

Delivery coordination

Clear hand-offs and status updates for approved, medically compliant transport.
